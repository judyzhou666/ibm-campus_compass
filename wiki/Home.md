# Campus Compass

Campus Compass helps students discover campus opportunities and build a weekly plan around their interests and availability. It is our project for the IBM SkillsBuild AI Experiential Learning Lab.

All events, students and schedules are **fictional**. The website runs on your own computer.

## What the AI Planner uses

- **By default: built-in rules** ("Rust planner · Simulated"). No IBM account or key is needed. This is the safest choice for a demo.
- **Optional: IBM watsonx.ai with the Granite model** ("Rust planner · IBM watsonx"). It understands the student's message; our own rules still choose and check every event. If watsonx is unavailable, the app falls back to the built-in rules and says so.
- **IBM Bob** is the AI coding assistant we use to **build** the project. The website never calls Bob.
