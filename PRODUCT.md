# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
University students discovering campus opportunities; team members demonstrating Campus Compass for the IBM AI Experiential Learning Lab.
## Product Purpose
Turn interests, practical constraints and weekly availability into a small feasible plan. Student approves every addition.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Fictional events and schedules are authorized by the user. No real calendar, Canvas or personal information integrations. Chatbot name undecided; use AI Planner. IBM Bob contribution is required for the course but has not been performed by this implementation. Chat is initially a clearly labelled deterministic simulation; the user has watsonx access and explicitly requested deferring its integration.
## Brand Commitments
Preserve the supplied green and white dashboard, sidebar navigation, weekly calendar, event cards, and chat plus draft-plan layout. Improve density and usability within that established design.
## Stack
Rust backend with Axum, Tokio and Serde; React/TypeScript frontend built with Vite. Shared fictional catalog, browser-local demo state, no live IBM credentials required.
## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied product proposal, IBM lab guide, and two dashboard/planner reference images. No real event dataset or IBM credentials supplied. The user also supplied the cat mascot for use in this website.
